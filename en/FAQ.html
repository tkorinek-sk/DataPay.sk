ACCOUNTING

Accounting Questions

Information about pricing, accounting entries, documents and the scope of our accounting services.

An accounting entry is one record in the accounting journal or the relevant accounting records. The number of accounting entries may therefore differ from the number of invoices or documents.

The main factors are the number of accounting entries per month and the complexity of your accounting needs. If you do not know your entry count, simply tell us about your business and document volume, and we will help you select the right package.

If you exceed the monthly limit, additional entries are charged at the applicable per-entry rate stated in the current price list.

If your business regularly exceeds the limit, we can agree on a more suitable service package.

Pricing depends on the scope of accounting work and the number of entries processed. If the number of entries or the workload changes significantly, the final fee may be adjusted accordingly.

Single-entry and double-entry bookkeeping are different methods of maintaining accounting records. Which method applies depends mainly on the legal form and nature of your business.

If you are unsure which accounting method applies to your business, we will be happy to help.
